FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I create an event on Vibent?
Creating an event is easy! Sign up for a free account, click "Create Event," fill in the details (title, date, location, ticket types), and publish. Your event goes live immediately and starts appearing in search results.
What are the fees for selling tickets?
Vibent charges a small service fee per ticket sold. Free events have no fees. Check our pricing page for a complete breakdown of our transparent fee structure.
How do I get a refund?
Refund policies are set by event organizers. If you need a refund, contact the organizer directly through the event page. If you have issues, our support team is here to help mediate.
Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?
Yes! You can transfer your ticket from your account settings. Go to "My Tickets," select the ticket, and click "Transfer." The new recipient will receive the ticket via email.
How do I contact support for a specific event?
On each event page, you'll find a "Contact Organizer" button. This sends your message directly to the event host. For platform-related issues, use the form above or email us directly.
