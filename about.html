Who We Are

Making Every Moment Count Since 2024

Vibent was born from a simple idea: finding and attending great events shouldn't be complicated. We believe that shared experiences — whether a tech conference, a jazz night, or a food festival — are what make life rich.

Founded in 2024, we've grown from a small side project to a platform serving over 50,000 event-goers across 180+ cities. Our team is passionate about building tools that help organizers succeed and attendees discover their next favorite event.

We're not just a ticketing platform. We're a community of creators, explorers, and experience-seekers who believe the best memories are made when people come together.
